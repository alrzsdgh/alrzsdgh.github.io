About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at Clemson University in South Carolina, working as a Graduate Research Assistant with Prof. Wael AbdAlmageed. My research sits between causal representation learning and AI for healthcare: I build models that learn disentangled, causally meaningful representations from images, text and time series, and turn them into diagnostic tools clinicians can trust.

Before Clemson, I spent three years as a research assistant at Sari Medical Center, developing deep learning systems for 12-lead ECG analysis, CT-based COVID-19 diagnosis and parasite detection in microscopy. I hold an M.Sc. in Mechatronics Engineering from the University of Tehran and a B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic).

Projects

Causal ML · Time Series

GranTVAE

Integrates Granger causality into a Transformer-based VAE, so the model discovers causal structure and forecasts multichannel time series together. Robust to noise, irregular sampling and missing data.

Repository →

Causal ML · Vision-Language

Causal Multimodal Generative Model

Combines images with VLM-generated descriptions to learn disentangled latent factors, infer how they cause each other, and generate counterfactual images.

Cardiology · ECG

3DECG-Net

Turns 12-lead ECGs into 3D recurrence-plot volumes and classifies seven arrhythmias with a Conv-LSTM residual network and multi-head attention. LIME explains each lead's contribution.

Repository →

Radiology · CT

MASERes for COVID-19

A 3D Multi-Head Attention Squeeze-and-Excitation Residual Network for patient-level COVID-19 diagnosis from CT volumes, alongside slice-level transfer learning models.

Repository →

Parasitology · Microscopy

LeishFuNet

Detects Leishmania parasites in microscopic slides by fusing VGG19, ResNet50, MobileNetV2 and DenseNet169 with same-domain transfer learning, with Grad-CAM explanations for telemedicine.
